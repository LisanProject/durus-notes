Chapter 3: Bab al-Af'al (Verbs)

Having covered al-kalam, al-i'rab, and the mu'rabat, the text turns to the verb itself: what a verb is, its three types, and the rulings that apply to each. Since only the present tense (mudari') is actually declinable (mu'rab), this chapter's second half is given over entirely to the particles that push it out of its default raf' into nasb or jazm.

Here is a list of the sections covered so far in this chapter.

The Three Verb Types and Their Base Rulings: the definitions of ism and fi'l, the madi/mudari/amr distinction, the base ruling of each (mabni 'ala al-fath, mu'rab, mabni 'ala the jazm-sign), the four mudari' markers, and the amr/du'a/iltimas distinction by speaker rank.

The Ten Particles That Cause Nasb: the ten nawasib in full, with worked explanations of an, lan, idhan, and kay/lam kay.

The Eighteen Particles That Cause Jazm: the eighteen jawazim in full, worked explanations of lam, lamma, and alam, the signs of jazm, and the exception granted to poetry.
