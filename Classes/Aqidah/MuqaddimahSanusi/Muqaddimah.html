Imam al-Sanusi

Notes on Muqaddimat al-Sanusi, taught by Sidi Shaykh Abdullahi Ahmed. I joined this class fairly late and will need to update the notes from the earlier sessions, but the bulk of what I've learned so far covers the third through eighth preliminary discussions that ground the rest of the aqidah primer: free will and shirk, the roots of disbelief and innovation, the categories of existence and the divine attributes, the soul, and the foundations of accepting a transmitted report.

Topics

  1. Al-Muqaddimah al-Thalithah - mukhayyar and musayyar, the six types of shirk with their rulings, the true meaning of ibadah, hearing of the dead, and dreams as a source of knowledge.
  2. Al-Muqaddimah al-Rabi'ah - the seven roots of disbelief and innovation: al-ijab al-dhati, al-tahsin al-'aqli, taqlid radi, al-rabt al-'adi, jahl murakkab, bare literalism, and ignorance of reason and Arabic.
  3. Al-Muqaddimah al-Khamisah - the four categories of existence relative to place and specifier, jawhar and 'arad, and the Mu'tazilah-Ash'ari debate on the divine attributes and the Qur'an's createdness.
  4. Al-Muqaddimah al-Sadisah - the six mumkinat and mutaqabilat, the Ash'ari classification of the soul (al-ruh), and reflections on brokenness and sincerity.
  5. Al-Muqaddimah al-Sabi'ah - the seven eternal attributes of meaning: power, will, knowledge, life, hearing, sight, and speech, and their role in negating corporeality from Allah.
  6. Al-Muqaddimah al-Thaminah - khabar, insha, sidq, amanah, and the foundations of accepting a transmitted report, closing with the granting of ijazah.
