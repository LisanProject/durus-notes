The Small Abridgment on What Every Muslim Must Know, in Worship according to the School of Imam al-Shafi'i

Imam Abdullah ibn Abd al-Rahman Ba Fadl al-Hadrami, 850-918 AH

About These Notes

Mukhtasar al-Latif, also known as al-Mukhtasar al-Saghir, is a short primer in Shafi'i fiqh covering the acts of worship every Muslim needs: purification, prayer, zakah, fasting, and Hajj. These notes follow my study of the text with Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha al-Azhari. Each section gives the Arabic matn, a word by word vocabulary list, a translation, and the commentary from class, with the points the Shaykh stressed most set apart in boxes.

This is a working document. Errors will be present and corrections are welcomed. The reader is urged in all matters to return to a qualified teacher and to treat these notes as a companion to study, not a substitute for it.

Chapters

Here is a list of the chapters covered in this book.

Chapter 1: The Introduction covers the book and its author, the definition and areas of fiqh, the author's opening formula, and the knowledge every Muslim is obligated to learn.

Chapter 2: Taharah covers the definition of purification and the obligations of wudu.

More chapters will be added here as the lessons continue.
