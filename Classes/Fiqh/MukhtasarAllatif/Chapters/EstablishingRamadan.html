Establishing the Start of Ramadan ثُبُوتُ رَمَضَانَ

The Text

Text
[بَابُ الصِّيَامِ]
يَثْبُتُ دُخُولُ رَمَضَانَ بِاسْتِكْمَالِ شَعْبَانَ ثَلَاثِينَ، أَوْ رُؤْيَةِ الْهِلَالِ، وَيَكْفِي شَهَادَةُ عَدْلٍ.
Vocabulary
  • بَابُ الصِّيَامِthe chapter of fasting
  • يَثْبُتُis established
  • دُخُولُ رَمَضَانَthe entry of Ramadan
  • بِاسْتِكْمَالِ شَعْبَانَby completing Sha'ban
  • ثَلَاثِينَas thirty (days)
  • أَوْ رُؤْيَةِ الْهِلَالِor sighting the crescent
  • وَيَكْفِيand it suffices
  • شَهَادَةُ عَدْلٍthe testimony of one upright person
Translation
The chapter of fasting. The entry of Ramadan is established by completing Sha'ban as thirty days, or by sighting the crescent, and the testimony of one upright witness suffices.
Commentary

Linguistically, sawm or siyam means abstaining. In the Sharia it is abstaining from what breaks the fast, from dawn to sunset, with an intention, in a specified manner. Simply not eating is not a fast: the time and the intention matter.

Fasting was prescribed for nations before us:

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا كُتِبَ عَلَيْكُمُ الصِّيَامُ كَمَا كُتِبَ عَلَى الَّذِينَ مِنْ قَبْلِكُمْ

"O you who believe, fasting has been prescribed for you as it was prescribed for those before you." Maryam, peace be upon her, vowed a fast of silence. The particular form of fasting Ramadan from dawn to sunset with an intention for each day belongs to this Ummah.

Sawm (Fasting). Linguistically, abstaining. In the Sharia: abstaining from what breaks the fast, from dawn until sunset, with an intention, in a specified manner.

General and Individual Establishment

General (thubut 'amm)Individual (thubut khass)
BindsAll the Muslims of the landOnly that person
HowCompleting Sha'ban as thirty days, or a sighting testified to before the judge by one upright witnessSeeing the crescent himself, or being convinced by a report that does not meet the conditions of testimony

An upright witness ('adl) is one who has not committed a major sin and does not persist in a minor one. He must also be legally responsible (adult and sane), male, free, of sound judgment, able to hear, and able to see. The report of a sinful person, a woman, a child, a slave, or a blind or deaf person does not establish Ramadan for everyone, but whoever is convinced it is true is bound by it himself.

If the judge declares the next day the thirtieth of Sha'ban, but a person sees the crescent himself, he must fast.

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that general establishment, by completing Sha'ban or a testified sighting, binds the community, while individual establishment binds only the one who saw the crescent or was convinced by a report.
