Delaying, Determining the Time, and Making Up Prayers التَّأْخِيرُ وَالِاجْتِهَادُ وَالْقَضَاءُ

Letting Part of the Prayer Fall Outside the Time

Text
وَمَنْ أَخَّرَ الصَّلَاةَ حَتَّى وَقَعَ بَعْضُهَا خَارِجَ الْوَقْتِ بِغَيْرِ عُذْرٍ.. عَصَى.
Vocabulary
  • وَمَنْand whoever
  • أَخَّرَdelayed
  • الصَّلَاةَthe prayer
  • حَتَّىuntil
  • وَقَعَfell
  • بَعْضُهَاpart of it
  • خَارِجَoutside
  • الْوَقْتِthe time
  • بِغَيْرِ عُذْرٍwithout an excuse
  • عَصَىhas sinned
Translation
Whoever delays the prayer until part of it falls outside the time, without an excuse, has sinned.
Commentary

Letting the time run out without an excuse is prohibited and a major sin. The author says even part of the prayer falling outside the time, without an excuse, is sinful. Whether the prayer was missed with or without an excuse, it must be made up; what differs is how urgently.

Determining the Time

[الِاجْتِهَادُ فِي الْوَقْتِ]
وَمَنْ لَمْ يَعْرِفْ وَقْتَهَا.. وَجَبَ عَلَيْهِ أَنْ يَجْتَهِدَ فِي مَعْرِفَةِ دُخُولِهِ بِدِرَاسَةٍ أَوْ حِرْفَةٍ.
Vocabulary
  • الِاجْتِهَادُstriving to determine
  • فِي الْوَقْتِthe time
  • لَمْ يَعْرِفْdoes not know
  • وَقْتَهَاits time
  • وَجَبَ عَلَيْهِit is obligatory on him
  • أَنْ يَجْتَهِدَto strive, to use his judgment
  • فِي مَعْرِفَةِin knowing
  • دُخُولِهِits entry
  • بِدِرَاسَةٍby study, such as a known amount of reading
  • أَوْ حِرْفَةٍor a craft, a known amount of work
Translation
Striving to determine the time. Whoever does not know its time must strive to determine when it enters, by study or by a craft.
Commentary

The Shaykh described levels of knowing the time:

First: Direct knowledge. Seeing it yourself, such as watching the sun set, or the adhan of a trustworthy mu'adhdhin who knows the times. Not everyone who calls the adhan pays attention to the correct time, so both trustworthiness and knowledge matter.

Second: Ijtihad. If there is no direct knowledge and no reliable adhan, estimate. For instance, by how long it takes to read a known amount of Quran, or to complete a known amount of work.

(1)

One does not move to the second level while the first is available. If a reliable, knowledgeable mu'adhdhin calls the adhan, rely on it rather than estimating by how many sections you have read.

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that estimating is only for when direct knowledge or a reliable adhan is unavailable, never a substitute for them.

(2)

Making Up a Prayer Missed Without Excuse

وَمَنْ فَاتَهُ فَرْضٌ بِغَيْرِ عُذْرٍ.. وَجَبَ عَلَيْهِ قَضَاؤُهُ عَلَى الْفَوْرِ.
Vocabulary
  • فَاتَهُhe missed
  • فَرْضٌan obligatory prayer
  • بِغَيْرِ عُذْرٍwithout an excuse
  • وَجَبَ عَلَيْهِit is obligatory on him
  • قَضَاؤُهُto make it up
  • عَلَى الْفَوْرِimmediately
Whoever misses an obligatory prayer without an excuse must make it up immediately.
Commentary

A prayer missed without excuse must be made up immediately ('ala al-fawr). A prayer missed with an excuse, such as sleep or forgetting, must still be made up, but there is latitude in when ('ala al-tarakhi).

Someone who neglected prayer for a year and then repented must count and make up what he missed. Repentance does not cancel what is owed, just as it does not cancel unpaid zakah or missed fasts. The same applies to someone who reached adulthood without learning to pray and only later began.

In the position the Shaykh taught, a person with many missed prayers must use his time to make them up, not wasting it, apart from real needs such as work, sleep, and eating. He also reported that some scholars allow more latitude, making them up as one is able. Both views agree they must be made up.

'Ala al-Fawr (Immediately). To be done now, without delay. Applies to a prayer missed without excuse.

'Ala al-Tarakhi (With Latitude). Still owed, but may be done over time. Applies to a prayer missed with an excuse.

(3)

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that repentance does not erase missed prayers. They remain a debt to be made up.

(4)
