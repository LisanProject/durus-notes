The Integrals of Hajj فُرُوضُ الْحَجِّ

The Text

Text
[فَصْلٌ فِي فُرُوضِ الْحَجِّ]
فُرُوضُ الْحَجِّ خَمْسَةٌ:
١- الْإِحْرَامُ، وَهُوَ النِّيَّةُ بِالْقَلْبِ.
٢- وَالْوُقُوفُ بِعَرَفَةَ.

٤- وَالسَّعْيُ.
٥- وَالْحَلْقُ أَوِ التَّقْصِيرُ.

Translation
A section on the integrals of Hajj. They are five: ihram, which is the intention in the heart; standing at Arafah; the tawaf after the standing; the sa'y; and shaving or shortening the hair.

Fard and Wajib in Hajj

In other chapters "fard" covers both conditions and integrals. In the chapter of Hajj, fard and wajib are two separate categories:

Fard (integral)Wajib (obligation)
If left outNo valid Hajj until it is doneHajj is valid
Repaired by a sacrifice (dam)?NoYes
SinIf left without an excuse

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that in Hajj, leaving a fard means there is no valid Hajj and no sacrifice can repair it, while leaving a wajib leaves the Hajj valid but requires a sacrifice, and is sinful without an excuse.

(1)

The Five Integrals

1. Ihram: the intention in the heart to perform Hajj: "I intend the obligatory Hajj." Saying it aloud is recommended. Ihram for Hajj may only be entered in its months: Shawwal, Dhu al-Qa'dah, and the first ten days of Dhu al-Hijjah, until dawn on the Day of Sacrifice. Ihram for Hajj in Ramadan, or on the Day of Sacrifice, is not valid as Hajj. Being allowed to enter ihram in Shawwal does not mean the rites begin then.

2. Standing at Arafah: being present anywhere within Arafah, standing, sitting, or asleep, even for a moment, while sane. Its time runs from zawal on the 9th of Dhu al-Hijjah until dawn on the 10th (roughly noon to 5 a.m.). Arriving before zawal, he stays until after it.

3. Tawaf al-Ifadah, the tawaf after Arafah. Three tawafs must be kept apart:

TawafWhenRuling
Al-QudumOn arrivalSunnah
Al-IfadahAfter ArafahFard: no Hajj without it
Al-Wada'Before leaving MakkahWajib: a sacrifice if left

4. Sa'y between Safa and Marwah. Its conditions, and those of tawaf, come later in the book.

5. Removing the hair: at least three hairs, by shaving, cutting, plucking, or burning. Shaving the whole head is better for men; shortening is better for women. Unlike wiping in wudu, where one hair within the head's boundary suffices, the three hairs may be taken from what hangs beyond it.

The Shaykh added order as a sixth integral. It applies among most of them, but not between the hair and the tawaf: either may come first, though removing the hair first is the sunnah.

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that standing at Arafah means being present there in its time, not standing upright, and that Tawaf al-Qudum, al-Ifadah, and al-Wada' are sunnah, fard, and wajib.

(2)

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that at least three hairs are removed for Hajj, unlike wiping the head in wudu, and that there is no required order between removing the hair and the tawaf.

(3)

Questions and Answers

Question: If ihram may be entered from Shawwal, when do the rites begin?

Answer: In the days of Hajj, not in Shawwal. One might arrive on the 9th, perform Tawaf al-Qudum and, if able, the sa'y, then go to Arafah.

Question: Is a child's Hajj valid, and must he perform it again as an adult?

Answer: It is valid and rewarded, but voluntary. As an adult who is able, he must perform the obligatory Hajj.
