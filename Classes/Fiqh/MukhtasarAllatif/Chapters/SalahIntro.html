Chapter 3: Salah بَابُ الصَّلَاةِ

This chapter covers prayer, the best of the bodily acts of worship. It opens with the five prescribed prayers and the gravity of praying them outside their times, then sets out the time of each prayer, how to determine the time and make up what is missed, the times in which voluntary prayer is prohibited, those upon whom prayer is obligatory, and the conditions, obligations, and sunnahs of prayer.

Here is a list of the sections covered in this chapter.

The Five Prescribed Prayers covers the meaning of salah, its rank among acts of worship, why the obligatory prayers are five, the major sin of praying outside the time, and the valid excuses of sleep, forgetfulness, and combining.

The Prayer Times covers the beginning and end of each of the five times, the shadow of istiwa', the old and new positions on Maghrib, the true and false dawn, praying at the start of the time, and delaying Dhuhr in intense heat.

Delaying, Determining the Time, and Making Up Prayers covers letting a prayer fall outside its time, how to determine the time when unsure, and making up missed prayers immediately or with latitude.

The Times When Prayer Is Prohibited covers the five prohibited times and which prayers they apply to, according to whether a prayer has a preceding, concurrent, or later cause, the Friday khutbah, and the exceptions of Friday and Makkah.

Upon Whom Prayer Is Obligatory covers the four conditions of the obligation, who makes up missed prayers and who does not, commanding children to pray at seven and ten, and when an impediment ends or begins within the time.

Teaching Children What They Will Need covers parents' duty to teach the obligations and prohibitions before puberty, the signs of puberty, and who pays for a child's teacher.

The Conditions of Prayer covers the six conditions of validity: the time, the qiblah and its travel exception, the 'awrah and what counts as a covering, purity from hadath and najasah, knowing the obligations, and excused najasah.

The Obligations of Prayer covers the seventeen integrals and why the books count them differently, from the intention to order, and what happens when an integral is put out of order.

The Sunnahs of Prayer covers the ab'ad and hay'at, the opening supplication, recitation, aloud and silent, the qunut, the tasbihs, the sitting of rest, iftirash and tawarruk, and khushu'.

What Invalidates the Prayer covers speech, eating, and movement, and how much of each invalidates.

The Prostration of Forgetfulness covers its four causes, the integrals, ab'ad, and hay'at when omitted, and following the imam.

The Prostration of Recitation covers its fourteen places, its conditions outside prayer, and its rulings within prayer.

Whom One May Not Follow covers the ummi, discovering the imam's hadath or disbelief, and which pairings of men, women, and the khuntha are valid.

The Conditions of Following an Imam covers the ruling of congregation and the six conditions of following.

Shortening the Prayer While Traveling covers the distance, where travel begins, prohibited journeys, the wanderer, praying behind one praying in full, and making up prayers.

Combining Prayers While Traveling covers combining forward and by delay, and how their conditions differ.

Upon Whom Jumu'ah Is Obligatory, and the Excuses covers who must attend and the excuses for missing Jumu'ah and congregation.

The Conditions of Jumu'ah covers its time, place, one Jumu'ah per town, congregation, and the forty.

The Two Sermons covers the five essentials of the khutbahs and their conditions.

Preparing the Deceased covers the communal obligation, the martyr, the non-Muslim, the miscarried fetus, washing, and shrouding.

The Funeral Prayer covers its seven integrals, the intention, placing al-Fatihah, and a fifth takbir.

Burial and Mourning covers the minimum and complete grave, the qiblah, and prohibited lamentation.
