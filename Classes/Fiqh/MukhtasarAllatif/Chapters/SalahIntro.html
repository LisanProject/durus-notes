Chapter 3: Salah بَابُ الصَّلَاةِ

This chapter covers prayer, the best of the bodily acts of worship. It opens with the five prescribed prayers and the gravity of praying them outside their times, then sets out the time of each prayer, how to determine the time and make up what is missed, the times in which voluntary prayer is prohibited, those upon whom prayer is obligatory, and the conditions and obligations of prayer.

Here is a list of the sections covered in this chapter.

The Five Prescribed Prayers covers the meaning of salah, its rank among acts of worship, why the obligatory prayers are five, the major sin of praying outside the time, and the valid excuses of sleep, forgetfulness, and combining.

The Prayer Times covers the beginning and end of each of the five times, the shadow of istiwa', the old and new positions on Maghrib, the true and false dawn, praying at the start of the time, and delaying Dhuhr in intense heat.

Delaying, Determining the Time, and Making Up Prayers covers letting a prayer fall outside its time, how to determine the time when unsure, and making up missed prayers immediately or with latitude.

The Times When Prayer Is Prohibited covers the five prohibited times and which prayers they apply to, according to whether a prayer has a preceding, concurrent, or later cause, the Friday khutbah, and the exceptions of Friday and Makkah.

Upon Whom Prayer Is Obligatory covers the four conditions of the obligation, who makes up missed prayers and who does not, commanding children to pray at seven and ten, and when an impediment ends or begins within the time.

Teaching Children What They Will Need covers parents' duty to teach the obligations and prohibitions before puberty, the signs of puberty, and who pays for a child's teacher.

The Conditions of Prayer covers the six conditions of validity: the time, the qiblah and its travel exception, the 'awrah and what counts as a covering, purity from hadath and najasah, knowing the obligations, and excused najasah.

The Obligations of Prayer covers the seventeen integrals and why the books count them differently: the intention, the opening takbir, standing, al-Fatihah and its substitutes, ruku', i'tidal, and tuma'ninah.

More sections will be added here as the lessons continue.
