Chapter 1: The Introduction

This chapter covers everything before the first chapter of the matn. It introduces the book and its author, defines fiqh as a discipline and maps out its four areas, then walks through the author's opening formula and his statement of purpose: that this is the knowledge every Muslim cannot do without. The opening is short, but Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha uses it to set out principles that run through the whole book, such as judging by the outward, reading words in their context, and learning a ruling before it becomes due.

Here is a list of the sections covered in this chapter.

The Book, the Author, and the Science of Fiqh covers the titles of the book, the life and works of Ba Fadl al-Hadrami, the hadith on understanding the religion, the definition of fiqh, and its four major areas.

The Author's Opening covers the opening formula word by word: hamd, rabb, the testimony and outward rulings, the name Muhammad, who the al and the Companions are, and the transition wa ba'd.

Knowledge Every Muslim Must Have covers the author's statement of purpose, what individually obligatory knowledge means, how it depends on each person's circumstances, and why the student of knowledge studies more.
