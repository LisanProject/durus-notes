Combining Prayers While Traveling الْجَمْعُ لِلْمُسَافِرِ

The Text

Text
[فَصْلٌ فِي الْجَمْعِ لِلْمُسَافِرِ]
وَيَجُوزُ لِلْمُسَافِرِ سَفَرًا طَوِيلًا مُبَاحًا الْجَمْعُ بَيْنَ الظُّهْرِ وَالْعَصْرِ تَقْدِيمًا أَوْ تَأْخِيرًا، أَوْ بَيْنَ الْمَغْرِبِ وَالْعِشَاءِ.
وَإِذَا جَمَعَ التَّقْدِيمَ.. يُشْتَرَطُ: الْبُدَاءَةُ بِالْأُولَى، وَنِيَّةُ الْجَمْعِ فِيهَا، وَأَلَّا يَطُولَ الْفَصْلُ بَيْنَهُمَا.
وَفِي جَمْعِ التَّأْخِيرِ يُشْتَرَطُ: أَنْ يَنْوِيَ التَّأْخِيرَ قَبْلَ خُرُوجِ وَقْتِ الظُّهْرِ وَقَبْلَ خُرُوجِ وَقْتِ الْمَغْرِبِ.

Translation
A section on combining for the traveler. A traveler on a long, permissible journey may combine Dhuhr and Asr, bringing the second forward or delaying the first, and likewise Maghrib and Isha. When he combines by bringing forward, the conditions are: beginning with the first prayer, intending the combination in it, and not leaving a long gap between them. In combining by delay, the condition is that he intend the delay before the time of Dhuhr ends, and before the time of Maghrib ends.
Commentary

Jam' Taqdim (Combining Forward). Praying the second prayer in the first one's time: Dhuhr then Asr in Dhuhr's time.

Jam' Ta'khir (Combining by Delay). Delaying the first prayer into the second one's time: Dhuhr and Asr in Asr's time.

(1)

Only two pairs combine: Dhuhr with Asr, and Maghrib with Isha. Fajr is never combined, and Asr is never combined with Maghrib. The journey must meet the same two conditions as shortening: long and not prohibited.

"Permitted" signals that praying each in its own time is generally better, to stay clear of disagreement: Imam Abu Hanifa held combining invalid except at Arafah and Muzdalifah in Hajj. Combining is better at Arafah and Muzdalifah, for someone a travel group follows, and when a group is combining and praying alone would mean missing the congregation.

Combining Forward

  • Begin with the first prayer: Dhuhr before Asr, Maghrib before Isha.
  • Intend combining during the first prayer: unlike shortening, this intention need not be at the opening takbir; it may come anywhere in the first prayer, even near its end.
  • No long gap: a short pause, such as a drink, is fine; a gap as long as two rak'ahs ends the combination.
  • The travel still present when the second prayer begins: if he arrives home after the first and before starting the second, he cannot combine; if travel ends after he has begun the second, he completes it.

Combining by Delay

  • Intend the delay in the first prayer's time, while more time remains than the prayer itself needs; if only enough time to pray it is left, the delay no longer counts as combining.
  • The travel must last until the second prayer is finished. Arriving home during Asr means it was not a valid delayed combination.
  • Order and continuity are recommended, not required: he may rest, eat, or drink between them, and may even pray the later one first.

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that the two kinds of combining differ in when the intention is made, whether order and continuity are required, and how long the travel must last: until the second prayer begins when combining forward, and until it ends when combining by delay.
