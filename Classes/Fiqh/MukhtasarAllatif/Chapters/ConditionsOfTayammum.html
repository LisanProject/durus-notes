The Conditions of Tayammum شُرُوطُ التَّيَمُّمِ

Aiming for the Earth

Text
فَصْلٌ فِي شُرُوطِ التَّيَمُّمِ
وَشُرُوطُ التَّيَمُّمِ: الْقَصْدُ إِلَى التُّرَابِ.
Vocabulary
  • وَشُرُوطُand the conditions of
  • التَّيَمُّمِtayammum
  • الْقَصْدُdeliberately aiming
  • إِلَىfor
  • التُّرَابِthe earth
Translation
A section on the conditions of tayammum. The conditions of tayammum are: deliberately aiming for the earth.
Commentary

Earth simply landing on the face is not enough. If the wind blows dust onto someone who did not intend it, that is not tayammum. The verse says "fa-tayammamu sa'idan tayyiban", seek out clean earth, and the word itself means deliberately heading for it.

Pure Earth with Dust, in Two Strikes

وَأَنْ يَكُونَ التُّرَابُ طَاهِرًا طَهُورًا لَهُ غُبَارٌ خَالِصًا عَنِ الْخَلِيطِ، وَأَنْ يَكُونَ بِضَرْبَتَيْنِ؛ ضَرْبَةٍ لِلْوَجْهِ، وَضَرْبَةٍ لِلْيَدَيْنِ.
Vocabulary
  • وَأَنْ يَكُونَand that it be
  • التُّرَابُthe earth
  • طَاهِرًاpure, not impure
  • طَهُورًاpurifying, not already used
  • لَهُ غُبَارٌhaving dust
  • خَالِصًاfree
  • عَنِ الْخَلِيطِof any admixture
  • بِضَرْبَتَيْنِwith two strikes
  • ضَرْبَةٍa strike
  • لِلْوَجْهِfor the face
  • لِلْيَدَيْنِfor the arms
Translation
And that the earth be pure, purifying, with dust, and free of admixture; and that it be done with two strikes, one for the face and one for the arms.
Commentary

It must be earth, not ash, plaster, cement, or anything else. Pure means free of impurity. Purifying means unused: earth that falls off during tayammum is not reused, and earth used in washing away a dog's impurity is also considered used. It must not be mixed with ash or plaster, since that can keep the earth from reaching the skin, and it must have dust; wet earth without dust does not work. Any color of earth is fine.

The Shaykh corrected his own wording during the lesson: used earth is not earth that "lifted hadath", since tayammum does not lift hadath, but earth used in a state of hadath.

The two strikes are two transfers of earth: one for the face, one for the arms. One strike cannot cover both. If the second does not give enough to cover the whole of the arms, another is taken.

Turab Musta'mal (Used Earth). Earth that has fallen off after tayammum, or earth used to remove impurity. It is not used again for tayammum.

(1)

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that it must be real earth with dust: not ash, plaster, cement, a mixture, or damp earth with no dust.

(2)

After the Time Has Entered, Renewed for Each Obligation

وَأَنْ يَكُونَ بَعْدَ دُخُولِ الْوَقْتِ.
وَأَنْ يُجَدِّدَ التَّيَمُّمَ لِكُلِّ فَرْضٍ.
Vocabulary
  • بَعْدَafter
  • دُخُولِthe entry of
  • الْوَقْتِthe prayer time
  • وَأَنْ يُجَدِّدَand that he renew
  • لِكُلِّfor every
  • فَرْضٍobligatory prayer
Translation
And that it be after the time has entered; and that he renew the tayammum for every obligatory prayer.
Commentary

If Dhuhr enters at one o'clock, a tayammum made at noon is not valid for it. Tayammum is a purification of necessity, and there is no necessity before the time. Wudu, by contrast, may be made before the time.

A person who prays Dhuhr with tayammum renews it for Asr once Asr enters. This applies even to a traveler combining two prayers: he renews tayammum for the second. Necessity is measured by its extent, and each individual obligation is its own necessity. The funeral prayer is different: it is a communal obligation, so one tayammum can be used for several funeral prayers if more deceased are brought.

Taharat Darurah (Purification of Necessity). The Shaykh's description of tayammum: a concession that only exists once the time has come and water cannot be used.

(3)

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes one tayammum per obligatory prayer, even when combining prayers on a journey.

(4)

Searching for Water

وَأَنْ يُفَتِّشَ عَنِ الْمَاءِ قَبْلَ التَّيَمُّمِ وَبَعْدَ دُخُولِ الْوَقْتِ فِي رَحْلِهِ وَرُفْقَتِهِ وَحَوَالَيْهِ، وَيُنَادِي: (مَنْ مَعَهُ مَاءٌ؟).
Vocabulary
  • وَأَنْ يُفَتِّشَand that he search
  • عَنِ الْمَاءِfor water
  • قَبْلَbefore
  • فِي رَحْلِهِin his belongings
  • وَرُفْقَتِهِand among his companions
  • وَحَوَالَيْهِand around him
  • وَيُنَادِيand he calls out
  • مَنْwho
  • مَعَهُhas with him
  • مَاءٌwater
Translation
And that he search for water before tayammum and after the time has entered, in his belongings, among his companions, and around him, and call out: "Who has water?"
Commentary

The verse says to make tayammum when you "do not find" water, and you cannot say you did not find it without looking. The search must be a real effort, not a glance around. How far one must go depends on what he knows:

Certain there is none. No search is needed.

Hadd al-Ghawth (Limit of Calling for Help). If water is only possibly or probably nearby, search within about 300 cubits, roughly 150 meters, the distance a call for help can be heard.

Hadd al-Qurb (Limit of Nearness). If certain water is nearby, go as far as about 2.5 kilometers.

Hadd al-Bu'd (Limit of Farness). Beyond that, there is no obligation to go, even if certain water is there.

(5)

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that the search comes after the time enters and must be a genuine effort, checking belongings, companions, and surroundings and asking those who might have water.

(6)

Having Neither Water Nor Earth

وَمَنْ لَمْ يَجِدْ مَاءً وَلَا تُرَابًا؛ كَأَنْ كَانَ عَلَى قُلَّةِ جَبَلٍ.. صَلَّى الْفَرْضَ وَحْدَهُ وَأَعَادَ.
Vocabulary
  • وَمَنْand whoever
  • لَمْ يَجِدْdoes not find
  • مَاءًwater
  • وَلَا تُرَابًاnor earth
  • كَأَنْ كَانَsuch as if he were
  • عَلَى قُلَّةِon the summit of
  • جَبَلٍa mountain
  • صَلَّىprays
  • الْفَرْضَthe obligatory prayer
  • وَحْدَهُalone, only
  • وَأَعَادَand repeats it
Translation
Whoever finds neither water nor earth, such as one on a mountaintop, prays the obligatory prayer only and repeats it later.
Commentary

This is faqid al-tahurayn, one who lacks both means of purification, such as someone stranded on a peak or confined without water or earth. He prays the obligatory prayer as he is, out of respect for the time, and repeats it later. He prays only the obligation: no voluntary prayers, prostration of recitation, or prostration of gratitude. If he is in major hadath, he recites only al-Fatihah, which the prayer requires, with no surah after it. In minor hadath, he still does not touch the mushaf.

He repeats the prayer when he finds water, or if he finds earth while the time remains. Finding earth after the time has ended will be covered in Matn Abi Shuja.

Faqid al-Tahurayn. One who has neither water nor earth. He prays the obligatory prayer only, as he is, and repeats it later.

(7)

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that this prayer is only for the obligatory prayer, and that one in major hadath recites only al-Fatihah.

(8)

Tayammum for Cold, and the Sinful Traveler

وَيَجُوزُ التَّيَمُّمُ لِلْبَرْدِ إِذَا فَقَدَ مَا يُسَخِّنُ بِهِ الْمَاءَ، أَوْ كَانَ لَا تَنْفَعُهُ تَدْفِئَةُ أَعْضَائِهِ بَعْدَ غَسْلِهَا، أَوْ لَا يَقْدِرُ عَلَيْهَا.
وَيَقْضِي الْمُتَيَمِّمُ لِلْبَرْدِ وَالْمُتَيَمِّمُ الْعَاصِي بِسَفَرِهِ.
Vocabulary
  • وَيَجُوزُand it is permitted
  • لِلْبَرْدِbecause of cold
  • إِذَا فَقَدَif he lacks
  • مَا يُسَخِّنُ بِهِsomething to heat with
  • الْمَاءَthe water
  • أَوْ كَانَor if
  • لَا تَنْفَعُهُdoes not help him
  • تَدْفِئَةُwarming
  • أَعْضَائِهِhis limbs
  • بَعْدَ غَسْلِهَاafter washing them
  • أَوْ لَا يَقْدِرُor he is not able
  • عَلَيْهَاto do it
  • وَيَقْضِيand repeats (the prayer)
  • الْمُتَيَمِّمُthe one who made tayammum
  • الْعَاصِيthe one sinning
  • بِسَفَرِهِby his journey
Translation
Tayammum is permitted because of cold if he has nothing to heat the water with, or warming his limbs after washing them would not help him, or he is unable to warm them. The one who made tayammum for cold, and the one who made tayammum while his journey itself is sinful, must repeat the prayer.
Commentary

In severe cold, if he cannot heat the water, and warming his limbs afterward would not prevent the harm or he cannot do it, he may make tayammum. If warming them would prevent harm, he must do that instead. Being merely able to pour cold water on oneself does not settle it if doing so would cause harm. He prays with tayammum and repeats the prayer later.

One whose journey itself is a sin, such as traveling to steal, does not get the concessions of travel: shortening prayer, tayammum, and wiping over leather socks. If he makes tayammum, he repeats the prayer. Likewise a sick person who is acting sinfully does not take the concession of tayammum until he repents.

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that the concessions of travel are not for one whose travel is itself sinful.

(9)

Removing Impurity First, and Partial Water

The Shaykh added that if a person can remove impurity from his body, he must do so before tayammum; tayammum made while removable impurity is on the body is invalid. If he cannot remove it, Ibn Hajar says he makes tayammum and repeats the prayer, while al-Ramli says he does not make tayammum but prays as one lacking both means and repeats. (This concerns impurity on the body, not on clothing.)

If someone has some water but not enough for complete wudu, he uses it for what he can, such as the face, and makes tayammum for the rest. He does not leave the water and make tayammum for everything.

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes using whatever water is available first, and making tayammum only for what it does not cover.

(10)

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha's advice on study: read the lesson before class, then repeat and review it after, so the rulings are understood and memorized. When a whole book is finished, such as the book of Taharah, set aside time to review it from start to finish.
