Making Up Fasts, and the Fidya الْقَضَاءُ وَالْفِدْيَةُ

The Text

Text
وَيَقْضُونَ كُلُّهُمْ، وَيَجِبُ مَعَ الْقَضَاءِ لِكُلِّ يَوْمٍ مُدٌّ عَلَى مَنْ أَفْطَرَ لِإِنْقَاذِ حَيَوَانٍ مُحْتَرَمٍ مُشْرِفٍ عَلَى الْهَلَاكِ، وَعَلَى الْحَامِلِ وَالْمُرْضِعِ إِذَا أَفْطَرَتَا خَوْفًا عَلَى الْوَلَدِ، وَعَلَى مَنْ أَخَّرَ الْقَضَاءَ إِلَى رَمَضَانَ آخَرَ بِغَيْرِ عُذْرٍ وَجَبَ مَعَ الْقَضَاءِ الْفِدْيَةُ، وَمَنْ أَفْطَرَ بِغَيْرِ عُذْرٍ.. وَجَبَ عَلَيْهِ الْقَضَاءُ عَلَى الْفَوْرِ.
Vocabulary
  • وَيَقْضُونَ كُلُّهُمْand they all make up
  • مَعَ الْقَضَاءِalong with the make-up
  • لِكُلِّ يَوْمٍ مُدٌّa mudd for each day
  • حَيَوَانٍ مُحْتَرَمٍa protected living being
  • خَوْفًا عَلَى الْوَلَدِout of fear for the child
  • أَخَّرَ الْقَضَاءَhe delayed the make-up
  • إِلَى رَمَضَانَ آخَرَuntil another Ramadan
  • بِغَيْرِ عُذْرٍwithout an excuse
  • الْفِدْيَةُthe fidya
  • عَلَى الْفَوْرِimmediately
Translation
They all make up the days. Along with the make-up, a mudd for each day is due from one who broke the fast to rescue a protected living being about to perish; from the pregnant or nursing woman if she broke it out of fear for the child; and from one who delayed the make-up until another Ramadan without excuse, who owes the fidya along with the make-up. Whoever broke the fast without excuse must make it up immediately.
Commentary

Qada' (Make-up). Fasting a missed day after Ramadan.

Fidya. One mudd of the land's staple food for each day, given to the poor or needy, not to all eight categories of zakah recipients.

(1)

Who Owes What

WhoMake-upFidya
Traveler, sick personYesNo
Pregnant or nursing woman fearing for herselfYesNo
Pregnant or nursing woman fearing for herself and the childYesNo
Pregnant or nursing woman fearing for the child aloneYesYes
One who broke it to rescue a protected lifeYesYes
One who delayed a make-up past the next Ramadan without excuseYesYes, for each year of delay
One whose excuse lasted the whole year until the next RamadanYes, after that RamadanNo

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that for the pregnant or nursing woman, fear for the child alone adds the fidya to the make-up, while fear for herself, with or without the child, means make-up only.

(2)

When the Make-Up Is Due

  • Missed with an excuse: made up after Ramadan and Eid, but not immediately.
  • Broken without an excuse: he abstains for the rest of that day, then makes it up immediately after Ramadan and Eid.

The same pattern runs through prayer and zakah: a duty delayed with an excuse is not urgent; without one, it is.

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes the difference between owing a make-up and owing it immediately: an excuse affects urgency, and an excuse lasting until the next Ramadan removes the fidya.

(3)

Questions and Answers

Question: If a missed fast is delayed for more than one year, does the fidya multiply?

Answer: Yes, it repeats for every year of delay. One day missed ten years ago is made up, with ten fidyas.
