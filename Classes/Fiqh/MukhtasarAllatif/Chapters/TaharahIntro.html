Chapter 2: Taharah بَابُ الطَّهَارَةِ

This chapter covers purification, the gateway to prayer. It begins with the legal definition of taharah and its four parts, then moves into wudu: its obligations, its sunnahs, what breaks it, and what becomes prohibited for one who has lost it. It continues with the etiquette of relieving oneself and then ghusl: what makes it obligatory, its obligations, and its sunnahs. Since prayer is not valid without purification, this is one of the chapters every Muslim is personally obligated to learn.

Here is a list of the sections covered in this chapter.

The Definition of Taharah covers the legal definition of purification, removing hadath, removing najasah, and what shares their meaning or form, with tayammum, istinja, and renewed wudu as examples.

The Obligations of Wudu covers the five legal rulings, the six obligations of wudu and their basis in Surat al-Ma'idah, the intention and when it must be made, and washing the face including the rulings on the beard.

The Sunnahs of Wudu covers the fifteen sunnahs listed in the matn, from the siwak and basmalah to continuity and not drying off, with when each one changes ruling and the single intention that covers them all.

The Nullifiers of Wudu covers the four things that break wudu, the sleep exception, contact between a man and a woman, who counts as a mahram, and touching the private part.

What Is Prohibited Without Wudu covers prayer, tawaf, touching and carrying the mushaf, the four cases of carrying it among belongings, and the discerning child.

The Etiquette of Relieving Oneself covers entering and leaving, where one should not relieve oneself and why, speaking, istibra', and the supplications on entering and leaving.

What Makes Ghusl Obligatory covers the five causes of ghusl and how to distinguish mani from madhy and wady.

The Obligations and Sunnahs of Ghusl covers the intention of ghusl and how it differs from wudu, washing every hair and all the skin, and the sunnahs of ghusl.

More sections will be added here as the lessons continue.
