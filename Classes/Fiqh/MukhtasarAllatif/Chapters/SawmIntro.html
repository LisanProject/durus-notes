Chapter 5: Sawm بَابُ الصِّيَامِ

This chapter covers fasting: how Ramadan is established, the intention, what breaks the fast, the conditions of validity and obligation, who may break the fast, making up and the fidya, and i'tikaf.

Here is a list of the sections covered in this chapter.

Establishing the Start of Ramadan covers completing Sha'ban, sighting the crescent, and general and individual establishment.

The Intention of Fasting covers the night intention and specification for obligatory fasts, and the daytime intention for voluntary ones.

What Breaks the Fast covers intercourse and its expiation, ejaculation, vomiting, and a substance reaching the interior.

Further Conditions, and Days Fasting Is Prohibited covers Islam, sanity, and purity all day, the Eids, the Days of Tashriq, and the latter half of Sha'ban.

Who Must Fast, and Who May Break the Fast covers the conditions of obligation, children, and the traveler, the sick, the pregnant or nursing woman, and the rescuer.

Making Up Fasts, and the Fidya covers who makes up, who also owes the fidya, and when the make-up is due.

I'tikaf covers its pillars and conditions, what counts as a masjid, and its rulings.

Review of Bab al-Siyam gathers the whole chapter: lists, conditions, make-up and fidya, commonly confused cases, and practice cases.

The chapter of fasting is complete.
