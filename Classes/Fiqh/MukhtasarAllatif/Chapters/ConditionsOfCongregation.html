The Conditions of Congregational Prayer شُرُوطُ الْجَمَاعَةِ

The Rulings of Congregation

Before the conditions, Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha set out how the ruling of praying in congregation varies:

RulingCases
Individually obligatoryJumu'ah, which is not valid alone
Communally obligatoryThe other obligatory prayers
RecommendedThe two Eids, both eclipse prayers, the rain prayer, Tarawih, Witr in Ramadan
Permissible, not specially recommendedDuha; the sunnahs before and after the obligatory prayers
Disliked, though validBehind an open sinner or an innovator
Contrary to what is preferable, though validA make-up prayer behind a prayer on time, or the reverse (missed Dhuhr behind people praying Asr)
Prohibited, though validOn usurped land
Prohibited and invalidBehind a prayer of a different form, such as the eclipse prayer behind an obligatory prayer

The Six Conditions

Text
[فَصْلٌ فِي شُرُوطِ الْجَمَاعَةِ]
وَشُرُوطُ الْجَمَاعَةِ سِتَّةٌ:
الْأَوَّلُ: أَلَّا يَتَقَدَّمَ عَلَى إِمَامِهِ، وَتُكْرَهُ مُسَاوَاتُهُ.
الثَّانِي: أَنْ يَجْمَعَهُمَا مَسْجِدٌ أَوْ فَضَاءٌ وَلَمْ يَزِدْ مَا بَيْنَهُمَا عَلَى ثَلَاثِ مِئَةِ ذِرَاعٍ.
الثَّالِثُ: أَنْ يَعْلَمَ بِانْتِقَالَاتِ الْإِمَامِ بِأَنْ يَرَاهُ أَوْ يَرَى بَعْضَ الْمَأْمُومِينَ، أَوْ يَسْمَعَهُ أَوْ يَسْمَعَ الْمُبَلِّغَ.
الرَّابِعُ: أَنْ يَنْوِيَ الِاقْتِدَاءَ بِهِ أَوِ الِائْتِمَامَ أَوِ الْجَمَاعَةَ.
الْخَامِسُ: أَنْ تُوَافِقَ صَلَاتُهُ صَلَاةَ إِمَامِهِ؛ فَلَا تَصِحُّ صَلَاةُ الْكُسُوفِ خَلْفَ صَلَاةِ الْمَكْتُوبَةِ، وَلَا تَصِحُّ الصُّبْحُ خَلْفَ الْجَنَازَةِ، وَلَا الْجَنَازَةُ خَلْفَ الصُّبْحِ.
السَّادِسُ: أَنْ يُتَابِعَهُ، فَلَوْ تَقَدَّمَ عَلَى إِمَامِهِ بِرُكْنَيْنِ فِعْلِيَّيْنِ، أَوْ تَخَلَّفَ عَنْهُ بِرُكْنَيْنِ فِعْلِيَّيْنِ طَوِيلَيْنِ بِغَيْرِ عُذْرٍ.. بَطَلَتْ صَلَاتُهُ، أَوْ تَخَلَّفَ عَنْهُ بِعُذْرِ بُطْءِ الْقِرَاءَةِ.. عُذِرَ إِلَى ثَلَاثَةِ أَرْكَانٍ طَوِيلَةٍ.

Translation
A section on the conditions of congregation. They are six. First: that the follower not be ahead of his imam; being level with him is disliked. Second: that a masjid or open space joins them, with no more than three hundred cubits between them. Third: that he know the imam's movements, by seeing him or some of the followers, or hearing him or the one relaying. Fourth: that he intend to follow him, take him as imam, or pray in congregation. Fifth: that his prayer agree with his imam's: the eclipse prayer is not valid behind an obligatory prayer, nor Subh behind a funeral prayer, nor a funeral prayer behind Subh. Sixth: that he follow him. If he gets ahead of his imam by two physical integrals, or falls behind him by two long physical integrals without an excuse, his prayer is invalid; if he falls behind for the excuse of slow recitation, he is excused up to three long integrals.

1. Not Ahead of the Imam

Standing ahead of the imam invalidates the prayer. Standing exactly level is disliked, and the reward of congregation is lost for the part prayed level. A single male follower stands to the imam's right, slightly behind; two or more stand behind him; a woman stands behind.

2. Joined by a Masjid or a Limited Distance

Inside one masjid, distance does not matter, even with the imam at one end and the follower at the far back. Outside a masjid, the gap may not exceed three hundred cubits unless the rows are connected, as when crowded rows spill into the streets on Laylat al-Qadr.

3. Knowing the Imam's Movements

By seeing the imam, seeing followers ahead, hearing the imam, or hearing a relayer (muballigh), who need not himself be praying. Amplification often makes a relayer unnecessary today.

4. Intending to Follow

The follower must intend following, the congregation, or taking him as imam. Joining the imam without that intention leaves the condition missing. The imam generally does not need an intention to lead, apart from certain cases.

5. The Two Prayers Agree in Form

Agreement means the same structure, not the same intention or number of rak'ahs. One may pray a two-rak'ah prayer behind a four or the reverse, missed Fajr behind Dhuhr, or Dhuhr behind Asr. The eclipse prayer, with two ruku' in a rak'ah, cannot be prayed behind an ordinary prayer or a funeral prayer.

Question: If the imam has begun Tarawih and I have not prayed Isha, may I join him intending Isha?

Answer: Yes. Each rak'ah of both has one ruku' and two prostrations. Intend Isha and following the imam, and when he gives salam after two rak'ahs, stand and complete Isha.

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that agreement between the two prayers is agreement in form, not in intention or number of rak'ahs.

6. Following the Imam

The best following is to begin each movement after the imam has completed his, while still catching him in the same integral; for example, starting down into sujud once his forehead is on the ground. Beginning before him is disliked.

CaseLimit
Getting ahead, without excuseTwo physical integrals invalidate (bowing while the imam stands is one; rising and going down to sujud before he reaches it makes two)
Falling behind, without excuseTwo physical integrals invalidate
Falling behind with an excuse (slow recitation, struggling with al-Fatihah, going back to correct a verse)Up to three long integrals; the short ones, i'tidal and the sitting between the prostrations, are not counted

The excuses for getting ahead are narrower: ignorance or forgetfulness.

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that without an excuse, two physical integrals ahead or behind invalidate the prayer, while an excused delay is allowed up to three long integrals, not counting the short ones.
