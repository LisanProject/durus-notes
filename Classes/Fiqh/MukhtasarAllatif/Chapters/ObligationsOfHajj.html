The Obligations of Hajj وَاجِبَاتُ الْحَجِّ

The Text

Text
[وَاجِبَاتُ الْحَجِّ]
وَوَاجِبَاتُهُ سِتَّةٌ:
١- الْإِحْرَامُ مِنَ الْمِيقَاتِ.
٢- وَالْمَبِيتُ بِمُزْدَلِفَةَ لَيْلَةَ النَّحْرِ.
٣- وَالْمَبِيتُ لَيَالِيَ التَّشْرِيقِ بِمِنًى.
٤- وَرَمْيُ جَمْرَةِ الْعَقَبَةِ يَوْمَ النَّحْرِ سَبْعَ حَصَيَاتٍ.
٥- وَرَمْيُ الْجِمَارِ الثَّلَاثِ كُلَّ يَوْمٍ مِنْ أَيَّامِ التَّشْرِيقِ بَعْدَ الزَّوَالِ، كُلَّ وَاحِدَةٍ سَبْعَ حَصَيَاتٍ، وَيَجُوزُ النَّفْرُ فِي الْيَوْمِ الثَّانِي قَبْلَ الْغُرُوبِ.
٦- وَطَوَافُ الْوَدَاعِ.

Translation
Its obligations are six: (1) ihram from the miqat; (2) staying at Muzdalifah on the night before the Day of Sacrifice; (3) staying at Mina on the nights of Tashriq; (4) stoning Jamrat al-Aqabah on the Day of Sacrifice with seven pebbles; (5) stoning the three jamrahs on each day of Tashriq after zawal, seven pebbles at each, and leaving on the second day before sunset is permitted; (6) the farewell tawaf.
Commentary

Each is a wajib: leaving one does not invalidate the Hajj, but a sacrifice (dam), such as a sheep, is owed, and leaving it without an excuse is sinful.

1. Ihram from the Miqat

Miqat can mean the time of a rite or its place; here it is the place.

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that the ruling for passing a miqat depends on whether he already intended Hajj or Umrah when he passed it.

2 and 3. The Nights at Muzdalifah and Mina

MuzdalifahMina
Which nightsThe night before the Day of SacrificeThe nights of the 11th, 12th, and 13th of Dhu al-Hijjah
How longA moment after the middle of the night sufficesMost of the night
Meaning of "staying"Being present, not necessarily sleeping

Leaving early: he may leave Mina on the 12th before sunset, and the night of the 13th and its stoning fall away. If the sun sets on the 12th while he is still there, he must stay the night.

فَمَنْ تَعَجَّلَ فِي يَوْمَيْنِ فَلَا إِثْمَ عَلَيْهِ وَمَنْ تَأَخَّرَ فَلَا إِثْمَ عَلَيْهِ

"Whoever hastens on in two days incurs no sin, and whoever stays on incurs no sin."

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that Muzdalifah needs only a moment after the middle of the night, while Mina needs most of the night.

(2)

4 and 5. The Stoning

DayPebblesTime
Day of Sacrifice (10th)7 at Jamrat al-AqabahFrom the middle of the night before; best from sunrise until zawal
Each day of Tashriq (11th, 12th, 13th)21: 7 each at the small, middle, and large jamrahAfter zawal; the last limit is sunset on the 13th
All four days70 in total (7 + 21 + 21 + 21)

The conditions of a valid stoning:

  • Order: the small, then the middle, then the large; never reversed.
  • Throwing: placing a pebble by hand is not throwing.
  • A stone or pebble.
  • Hitting the target area with certainty: if unsure it landed, throw again.
  • Aiming at the area, not the pillar, which only marks the place, and not a person.
  • Seven at each jamrah, no fewer.

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that stoning must be an actual throw aimed at the target area and landing in it with certainty, and that the pillar is only a marker.

(3)

6. The Farewell Tawaf

Tawaf al-Wada' is the last act before leaving Makkah. Leaving it does not invalidate the Hajj, but a sacrifice is owed, unlike Tawaf al-Ifadah, which is an integral.

Questions and Answers

Question: I passed the miqat without intending Hajj or Umrah, then decided to perform one. Must I go back?

Answer: No, since he had no intention when he passed it. One who passed already intending the rite owes a sacrifice.

Question: If I aim correctly but am unsure whether the pebble landed in the target, does it count?

Answer: No. It must land with certainty; if unsure, throw again.
