Zakah on Gold and Silver زَكَاةُ النَّقْدَيْنِ

The Text

Text
[فَصْلٌ فِي نِصَابِ الذَّهَبِ]
وَأَمَّا الذَّهَبُ.. فَنِصَابُهُ: عِشْرُونَ مِثْقَالًا. وَالْمِثْقَالُ: أَرْبَعَةٌ وَعِشْرُونَ قِيرَاطًا. وَنِصَابُ الْفِضَّةِ: مِئَتَا دِرْهَمٍ إِسْلَامِيٍّ مِنْ فِضَّةٍ خَالِصَةٍ، وَالدِّرْهَمُ الْإِسْلَامِيُّ: سَبْعَةَ عَشَرَ قِيرَاطًا إِلَّا خُمُسَ قِيرَاطٍ.
وَلَا تَجِبُ الزَّكَاةُ حَتَّى يَحُولَ عَلَيْهِ الْحَوْلُ، وَزَكَاتُهُ: رُبْعُ الْعُشْرِ. وَيُشْتَرَطُ فِي ذَلِكَ: أَلَّا يَكُونَ حُلِيًّا مُبَاحًا.
وَيَجِبُ فِي الْمَعْدِنِ مِنَ الذَّهَبِ وَالْفِضَّةِ: رُبْعُ عُشْرِهِ إِذَا كَانَ نِصَابًا فِي الْحَالِ.

Translation
A section on the nisab of gold. As for gold, its nisab is twenty mithqals, the mithqal being twenty-four qirats. The nisab of silver is two hundred Islamic dirhams of pure silver, the Islamic dirham being seventeen qirats less a fifth of a qirat. Zakah is not due until a year passes over it, and its zakah is a quarter of a tenth. A condition is that it not be permissible jewelry. On gold and silver from a mine, a quarter of a tenth is due immediately if it is a nisab.

The conditions are reaching the nisab and a full lunar year. What counts is the pure metal in an alloy. 21-karat gold is gold mixed with copper, so about 97 g of it holds the 84 g nisab. Mixing 100 g of gold with an equal weight of copper does not remove the 100 g, and lowering the karat to escape zakah is blameworthy.

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that zakah is reckoned on the pure gold in an alloy, so a mixture does not remove the obligation, and that the rate is a quarter of a tenth.

A gold patch on a vessel is prohibited; the details of patches, and of minerals and buried treasure, come in the following lesson.

Questions and Answers

Question: Does lower-karat gold escape zakah because it is not pure?

Answer: No. Calculate the pure gold it contains; if that reaches 84 g, zakah is due.
