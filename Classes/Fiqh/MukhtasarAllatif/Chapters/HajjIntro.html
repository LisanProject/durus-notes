Chapter 6: Hajj فَصْلٌ فِي الْحَجِّ

This chapter covers Hajj and Umrah: who must perform them, being able oneself or through another, the five integrals of Hajj, and its six obligations.

Here is a list of the sections covered in this chapter.

The Obligation of Hajj and Umrah covers who must perform Hajj and Umrah, being able oneself or through another, and Hajj for the deceased.

The Integrals of Hajj covers fard and wajib in Hajj, ihram, Arafah, the three tawafs, the sa'y, and removing the hair.

The Obligations of Hajj covers the miqats, the nights at Muzdalifah and Mina, the stoning, and the farewell tawaf.

More sections will be added here as the lessons continue.
