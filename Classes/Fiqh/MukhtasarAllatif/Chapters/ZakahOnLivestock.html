Zakah on Livestock زَكَاةُ الْمَاشِيَةِ

The Four Conditions

  1. Reaching the nisab: five camels, thirty cattle, forty sheep or goats.
  2. A full year (hawl) of ownership, by the lunar calendar. Livestock bought and sold within six, nine, or eleven months owes nothing on that basis.
  3. Grazing freely (sa'imah) on open pasture the whole year, without the owner paying for feed.
  4. Not working animals ('amilah): animals used to carry, plough, or draw water owe no zakah.

Nisab. The minimum quantity at which zakah becomes due.

Hawl. A full lunar year of ownership.

Waqs. The range in which the count rises but the amount due stays the same.

(1)

Question: I own five camels and one is used for work. Must all be working for the rule to apply?
Answer: No. The working camel is excluded, leaving four, below the nisab.

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes the four conditions of livestock zakah, that the year is lunar, and that the schedules must be memorized.

(2)

Camels

Text
فَأَمَّا الْإِبِلُ.. فَفِي خَمْسٍ مِنْهَا: شَاةٌ جَذَعَةٌ مِنَ الضَّأْنِ لَهَا سَنَةٌ، أَوْ ثَنِيَّةٌ مِنَ الْمَعْزِ لَهَا سَنَتَانِ، وَفِي عَشْرٍ: شَاتَانِ، وَفِي خَمْسَ عَشْرَةَ: ثَلَاثٌ، وَفِي عِشْرِينَ: أَرْبَعٌ، وَفِي خَمْسٍ وَعِشْرِينَ: بِنْتُ مَخَاضٍ لَهَا سَنَةٌ، وَفِي سِتٍّ وَثَلَاثِينَ: بِنْتُ لَبُونٍ لَهَا سَنَتَانِ، وَفِي سِتٍّ وَأَرْبَعِينَ: حِقَّةٌ لَهَا ثَلَاثُ سِنِينَ، وَفِي إِحْدَى وَسِتِّينَ: جَذَعَةٌ لَهَا أَرْبَعُ سِنِينَ، وَفِي سِتٍّ وَسَبْعِينَ: بِنْتَا لَبُونٍ، وَفِي إِحْدَى وَتِسْعِينَ: حِقَّتَانِ، وَفِي مِئَةٍ وَإِحْدَى وَعِشْرِينَ: ثَلَاثُ بَنَاتِ لَبُونٍ، ثُمَّ بَعْدَ هَذَا فِي كُلِّ أَرْبَعِينَ: بِنْتُ لَبُونٍ، وَفِي كُلِّ خَمْسِينَ: حِقَّةٌ.
Vocabulary
  • شَاةٌa sheep or goat
  • جَذَعَةٌ مِنَ الضَّأْنِa jadha'ah of sheep
  • ثَنِيَّةٌ مِنَ الْمَعْزِa thaniyyah of goats
  • بِنْتُ مَخَاضٍa one-year-old she-camel
  • بِنْتُ لَبُونٍa two-year-old she-camel
  • حِقَّةٌa three-year-old she-camel
  • جَذَعَةٌa four-year-old she-camel
  • فِي كُلِّ أَرْبَعِينَin every forty
  • وَفِي كُلِّ خَمْسِينَand in every fifty
Translation
As for camels: on five, one sheep, a one-year jadha'ah of sheep or a two-year thaniyyah of goats; on ten, two sheep; on fifteen, three; on twenty, four; on twenty-five, a bint makhad of one year; on thirty-six, a bint labun of two years; on forty-six, a hiqqah of three years; on sixty-one, a jadha'ah of four years; on seventy-six, two bint labun; on ninety-one, two hiqqah; on one hundred twenty-one, three bint labun. After that, in every forty a bint labun, and in every fifty a hiqqah.

Cattle

Text
وَأَمَّا الْبَقَرُ.. فَفِي ثَلَاثِينَ: تَبِيعٌ لَهُ سَنَةٌ، وَفِي أَرْبَعِينَ: مُسِنَّةٌ لَهَا سَنَتَانِ، ثُمَّ بَعْدَ هَذَا فِي كُلِّ ثَلَاثِينَ: تَبِيعٌ، وَفِي كُلِّ أَرْبَعِينَ: مُسِنَّةٌ.
Vocabulary
  • تَبِيعٌa one-year-old calf
  • مُسِنَّةٌa two-year-old cow
Translation
As for cattle: on thirty, a tabi' of one year; on forty, a musinnah of two years. After that, in every thirty a tabi', and in every forty a musinnah.

The tabi' is named for following its mother while grazing; the musinnah for its grown teeth.

Sheep and Goats

Text
وَأَمَّا الْغَنَمُ.. فَفِي أَرْبَعِينَ شَاةً: شَاةٌ، وَفِي مِئَةٍ وَإِحْدَى وَعِشْرِينَ: شَاتَانِ، وَفِي مِئَتَيْنِ وَوَاحِدَةٍ: ثَلَاثٌ، وَفِي أَرْبَعِ مِئَةٍ: أَرْبَعٌ، ثُمَّ فِي كُلِّ مِئَةٍ: شَاةٌ.
Translation
As for sheep and goats: on forty, one; on one hundred twenty-one, two; on two hundred one, three; on four hundred, four; then in every hundred, one.
