Chapter 4: Zakah بَابُ الزَّكَاةِ

This chapter covers zakah, the third pillar of Islam: what it is due on, its evidence and meaning, and the nisab, year, and rate for livestock, crops and fruits, gold and silver, mined gold and buried treasure, and trade goods, and zakat al-fitr.

Here is a list of the sections covered in this chapter.

The Obligation of Zakah covers what zakah is due on, its evidence, denying versus refusing it, and its meaning.

Zakah on Livestock covers the four conditions and the schedules for camels, cattle, and sheep.

Zakah on Crops and Fruits covers staples, when it becomes due, the nisab of 300 sa', the 10% and 5% rates, and combining harvests.

Zakah on Gold and Silver covers the nisab in grams, alloys, permissible jewelry, and mined gold and silver.

Zakah on Mined Gold and Silver, and Buried Treasure covers mined gold and silver, buried pre-Islamic treasure, their rates, and where treasure is found.

Zakah on Trade Goods covers when the year of trade goods begins, the nisab at year's end, and inherited stock.

Zakat al-Fitr covers when it is due, whom one pays for, the order when funds are short, the amount, and the staple food.

The chapter of zakah is complete.
