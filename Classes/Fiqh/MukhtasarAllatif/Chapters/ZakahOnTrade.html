Zakah on Trade Goods زَكَاةُ التِّجَارَةِ

The Text

Text
وَتَجِبُ زَكَاةُ التِّجَارَةِ إِذَا بَلَغَتْ نِصَابًا آخِرَ الْحَوْلِ، وَهِيَ: رُبْعُ عُشْرِ الْقِيمَةِ.
Vocabulary
  • زَكَاةُ التِّجَارَةِthe zakah of trade
  • إِذَا بَلَغَتْ نِصَابًاif it reaches a nisab
  • آخِرَ الْحَوْلِat the end of the year
  • رُبْعُ عُشْرِ الْقِيمَةِa quarter of a tenth of the value
Translation
The zakah of trade is due if the goods reach a nisab at the end of the year, and it is a quarter of a tenth of their value.
Commentary

Some books call it zakat al-'urud, the zakah of merchandise, as distinct from naqd, the monetary metals gold and silver.

When the Year Begins

  • Bought with the intention to trade: the year begins at the purchase.
  • Bought for use, such as a washing machine or refrigerator, or to preserve wealth: not trade goods, whatever their value.
  • Bought for use, later turned to trade: the year begins when the intention changes.
  • Inherited or received as a gift: not a purchase with trade intention. A son who inherits his father's supermarket starts a new year with his own intention; he does not continue his father's. Had he bought it from his father to trade, the year would start at the purchase.

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that the intention to trade starts the year, and that if the intention comes later, the year starts then.

(1)

The Nisab and the Rate

The nisab is the value of 85 g of gold, checked at the end of the year, not the beginning. The Shaykh used $10,000 only as an illustrative figure.

Example (assuming a $10,000 nisab)Result
Stock worth $9,000 at the start, $13,000 at year's endDue: 2.5% of $13,000
A $10,000 refrigerator bought for the homeNot due: not trade goods

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that the trade nisab is the value of 85 g of gold at the end of the year, and that $10,000 was only a rough example.

(2)

Questions and Answers

Question: If a person inherits a shop, does he continue the deceased owner's year?

Answer: No. The heir begins a new year with his own intention to trade.

Question: If stock is below the nisab at the start of the year but above it at the end, is zakah due?

Answer: Yes, because the nisab is assessed at the end of the year.
