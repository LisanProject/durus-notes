Review of Bab al-Zakah مُرَاجَعَةُ بَابِ الزَّكَاةِ

This page gathers the Book of Zakah in one place: the numbers to memorize, the conditions of each kind of wealth, the pairs most easily confused, and the points Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha stressed most. Each heading links back to the full section.

How to use this page Cover the tables and try to recite each nisab, rate, and condition before reading it. Then work through the cases before opening the answers.

Definitions to Memorize

TermDefinition
ZakahWhat is given from wealth or from the person, in a specified manner.
NisabThe minimum quantity at which zakah becomes due.
HawlA full lunar year of ownership.
WaqsThe range in which the count rises but the amount due stays the same.
Sa'imahLivestock grazing freely on open pasture all year.
QutA staple: food that sustains the body and is ordinarily stored.
Ma'dinThe mine, or what is extracted from it.
RikazGold or silver buried in the pre-Islamic era.
'UrudMerchandise held for trade.

Numbers to Have Cold

  • Camels: 5 to 24 give sheep (1 per 5); 25 bint makhad; 36 bint labun; 46 hiqqah; 61 jadha'ah; 76 two bint labun; 91 two hiqqah; 121 three bint labun; then 1 bint labun per 40, 1 hiqqah per 50.
  • Cattle: 30 tabi'; 40 musinnah; then a tabi' per 30, a musinnah per 40.
  • Sheep: 40 one; 121 two; 201 three; 400 four; then 1 per 100.
  • Crops: 1 sa' = 4 mudd; 1 wasq = 60 sa'; nisab 5 wasq = 300 sa' = 1,200 mudd.
  • Fitrah order when short: himself, wife, young child, father, mother, adult child.
  • Fitrah staples, highest first: wheat, barley, corn, rice.

Test Yourself: Cases to Work Through

Try each before opening the answer.

Case 1A man owns 130 camels grazing freely for a full year. What is due?

One hiqqah and two bint labun, splitting 130 as 50 + 40 + 40.

Case 2A farmer harvests 200 sa' of wheat in Rabi' and 150 sa' of wheat in Dhu al-Hijjah of the same year, all rain-fed. What is due?

The harvests combine to 350 sa', above the nisab, so a tenth is due.

Case 3A woman has kept 100 g of 21-karat gold bars for a year. Is zakah due?

Yes. The bars hold about 87.5 g of pure gold, above the nisab, and bars are not jewelry.

Case 4Digging on land he revived, a man finds a buried hoard of Roman-era silver coins above the nisab. What is due, and when?

A fifth, immediately. It is pre-Islamic silver found in land he revived.

Case 5A man owes fitrah for himself, his wife, two young children, and his mother, and has four sa'. For whom does he pay?

Himself, his wife, and his two young children. His mother comes after the young children.
