What Breaks the Fast الْمُفْطِرَاتُ

The Text

Text
وَشَرْطُ صِحَّةِ الصَّوْمِ: الْإِمْسَاكُ عَنِ الْجِمَاعِ عَمْدًا، وَعَنِ الِاسْتِمْنَاءِ، وَعَنِ الِاسْتِقَاءَةِ، وَعَنْ وُصُولِ عَيْنٍ إِلَى مَا يُسَمَّى جَوْفًا؛ كَبَاطِنِ أُذُنٍ أَوْ إِحْلِيلٍ مِنْ مَنْفَذٍ مَفْتُوحٍ، فَلَا يَضُرُّ وُصُولُ دُهْنٍ بِتَشَرُّبِ الْمَسَامِّ، وَلَا طَعْمِ الْكُحْلِ بِحَلْقِهِ.
وَلَا يُفْطِرُ إِذَا فَعَلَ ذَلِكَ جَاهِلًا أَوْ نَاسِيًا أَوْ مُكْرَهًا.
وَيُفْطِرُ بِخُرُوجِ الْمَنِيِّ بِلَمْسٍ بِلَا حَائِلٍ، أَوْ قُبْلَةٍ، أَوْ مُضَاجَعَةٍ، لَا بِفِكْرٍ وَنَظَرٍ.

  • الِاسْتِمْنَاءِcausing ejaculation by oneself
  • الِاسْتِقَاءَةِmaking oneself vomit
  • وُصُولِ عَيْنٍa tangible substance reaching
  • مَا يُسَمَّى جَوْفًاwhat is called an interior cavity

  • أَوْ إِحْلِيلٍor the urethra
  • مِنْ مَنْفَذٍ مَفْتُوحٍthrough an open passage
  • دُهْنٍoil
  • بِتَشَرُّبِ الْمَسَامِّby soaking through the pores
  • طَعْمِ الْكُحْلِ بِحَلْقِهِthe taste of kohl in his throat

Translation
A condition of a valid fast is abstaining from deliberate intercourse, from causing ejaculation, from making oneself vomit, and from any tangible substance reaching what is called an interior cavity, such as the inside of the ear or the urethra, through an open passage. Oil reaching inside by soaking through the pores does no harm, nor does the taste of kohl in the throat. The fast is not broken if he does any of this ignorant, forgetful, or coerced. It is broken by ejaculation from touching without a barrier, a kiss, or lying together, but not from thinking or looking.

Intercourse

Deliberate intercourse, knowing it is prohibited, by choice, carries five consequences:

  1. Sin
  2. Discretionary punishment (ta'zir) by the judge; if he repents before it is imposed, it falls away
  3. Making up the day
  4. Abstaining for the rest of that day
  5. The major expiation: freeing a slave; if unable, fasting two consecutive months; if unable, feeding sixty poor people

Excusable ignorance means a new Muslim, or someone raised far from scholars, such as an elderly person in a remote village who cannot use the internet. Someone living among scholars, or able to ask, is not excused.

Ejaculation and Vomiting

CaseBreaks the fast?
Deliberate self-stimulationYes
From touching, kissing, or lying together without a barrierYes
From contact through a barrierNo
From thinking or lookingNo
A wet dreamNo
Deliberately making oneself vomit, even if unchangedYes
Vomiting that overcomes himNo

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that not every ejaculation breaks the fast, and that deliberately making oneself vomit differs from being overcome by vomiting.

(1)

A Substance Reaching the Interior

  • A tangible substance ('ayn): even the size of a sesame seed or a small pebble. A smell or taste is an effect, not a substance.
  • The interior (jawf) is not only the stomach: the mouth, nose, ear, front passage, and back passage are open passages leading to it.
  • Not open passages: the eye, so eye drops and the taste of kohl do not break the fast; and the skin, so creams and oils absorbed through it do not.
  • Ignorant, forgetful, or coerced applies to all of the above: intercourse, ejaculation, vomiting, and a substance reaching the interior.

Sidi Shaykh Abdullah Taha emphasizes that a tangible substance differs from a smell or effect, and that the eye and the skin are not open passages.

(2)

Questions and Answers

Question: Does every ejaculation break the fast?

Answer: No. Only self-stimulation, or contact without a barrier. A wet dream, looking, thinking, or contact through a barrier do not.
