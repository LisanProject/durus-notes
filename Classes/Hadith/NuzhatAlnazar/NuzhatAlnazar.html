Liban's Online Notes

Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani

Nuzhat al-Nazar is Ibn Hajar's own commentary on his concise matn, Nukhbat al-Fikar, the most systematic and precise of the mustalah primers. These notes follow the commentary rather than the bare matn, since the author's own explanation of his terms is being studied directly. I am studying this text with Sidi Shaykh Muhyiddin Lashen, may Allah protect and preserve him. The edition is Dar al-Minhaj al-Qawim's, verified and annotated by Dr. Nur al-Din 'Itr, may Allah have mercy on him.

Chapters

Here is a list of the chapters covered in this book, following the order of the Nukhbah.

Chapter 1: Foundations of the Science covers the history of mustalah al-hadith, ijazah and transmission, the terms khabar, hadith, athar, and sunnah, and the epistemology of transmitted knowledge.

Chapter 2: The Khabar by Number of Routes covers mutawatir and the three kinds of ahad, the knowledge each yields, how ahad divides into accepted and rejected, and the two kinds of gharaba.

Chapter 3: The Accepted Hadith (al-Maqbul) covers sahih li-dhatihi, its conditions and ranks, the soundest chains, and the precedence of the two Sahihs.

  • Chapter 8: Knowing the Narrators (names and kunyas, tabaqat, the ranks of jarh and ta'dil, and the etiquette of teacher and student)
