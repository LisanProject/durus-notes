Review of Chapter 2: The Khabar by Number of Routes

How to use this page Recall each list before reading it, then work through "Asking Why," which takes the place of ta'lil in the fiqh review: why each clause of a definition is there, what it excludes (ihtiraz), and what each judgment rests on. The cases at the bottom test whether you can apply the reasons, not just repeat them.

The Map

TypeNarrators at the narrowest pointWhat it yields by itself
MutawatirA number custom rules out colluding on a lie, at every layerNecessary, certain knowledge ('ilm daruri)
Mashhur (mustafid)More than two, short of tawaturProbability (zann); knowledge only with qara'in
'AzizNever fewer than twoProbability
Gharib (fard)One, at any pointProbability

Everything below mutawatir is ahad (khabar al-wahid). Ahad then divides by acceptance: maqbul, mardud, and what is suspended (tawaqquf) until an indicator decides it.

Lists to Memorize

Four conditions of mutawatir
  • Kathrah such that custom rules out collusion or coincidence on a lie.
  • At every layer, from beginning to end.
  • Based on sense perception at its end (seen or heard), not on reasoning.
  • Producing knowledge in the one who hears it.
Two locations of gharaba
  • Fard mutlaq: at the root, where the Companion is. Example: the prohibition on selling wala', 'Abdullah ibn Dinar alone from Ibn 'Umar.
  • Fard nisbi: later in the chain. Example: the branches of faith, Abu Salih alone from Abu Hurayrah, then 'Abdullah ibn Dinar alone from Abu Salih.
Ahad surrounded by qara'in (yielding 'ilm nazari on the preferred view)
  • What both Shaykhs narrated, through their eminence, their precedence in sifting, and the ummah's reception, excluding what a hafiz criticized or what conflicts without preference.
  • Mashhur with distinct routes free of weakness and defects.
  • Musalsal bi al-a'immah: imams at every link, such as Ahmad from al-Shafi'i from Malik.
Examples to know by category
  • Mutawatir: the Hawd (50+ Companions), wiping over khuffs (70), the seven ahruf (27).
  • Mashhur and sahih: "None touches the Quran except one who is pure."
  • Mashhur and hasan: "No harm and no reciprocating harm."
  • Mashhur and da'if: "Seek knowledge even as far as China."
  • 'Aziz: "None of you believes until I am more beloved to him than his father and child."
  • Gharib yet sahih: "Actions are by intentions."

Asking Why: The Clauses of Each Definition

For each definition, ask what a clause is there to keep out. This is ihtiraz, the mustalah counterpart of asking what a condition in fiqh protects.

ClauseWhat it excludes or secures
Mutawatir: "a number custom rules out colluding on a lie"Any count that is large but still open to agreement or coincidence. The number matters only for what it produces.
Mutawatir: "from others like them, beginning to end"A report that is widespread in later generations but narrow at its source.
Mutawatir: "its basis is sense perception"A rational conclusion, however many hold it. Tawatur transmits what was seen or heard.
Mutawatir: "it produces knowledge in the hearer"A chain that looks sufficient on paper but does not actually yield certainty.
'Aziz: "not fewer than two from two"Reading it as "exactly two": more at some layers is fine; one anywhere makes it gharib.
Gharib: "at whatever point in the isnad"Limiting gharaba to the Companion level; this clause brings in the nisbi kind.
Khabar al-wahid: "whatever does not gather the conditions of tawatur"Defined by what it lacks, so it covers mashhur and 'aziz too, not just a single narrator.
Mustafid (minority view): "equal at beginning and end"Separates it from mashhur, which only requires more than two throughout.

Asking Why: What Each Judgment Rests On

Why does the narrowest point decide the classification? Because a chain is only as wide as its narrowest layer. Ten narrators later cannot undo a single narrator earlier; everything after him rests on him.

(1)

Why does mutawatir yield necessary knowledge and not nazari knowledge? Because a layperson with no capacity for inference still attains certainty from it. Nazari knowledge needs nazar, so certainty reached without it must be daruri.

(2)

Why are the conditions of mutawatir missing from the Nukhbah? Because the isnad sciences exist to test narrators so a report can be acted on or set aside, and the narrators of a mutawatir report are not tested this way. Acting on it is required without that investigation.

(3)

Why is no fixed number set for tawatur? Because the condition is the effect, certainty, not the count. Each proposed number came from one case where it produced knowledge; that does not make it hold everywhere.

(4)

Why is a suspended ahad report treated like the rejected? Not because rejection has been established, but because nothing has been found in it that requires acceptance. The suspension is a statement about the evidence, and later scholars may resolve it.

(5)

Why can the Sahihayn yield knowledge when their hadith are mostly ahad? Because qara'in surround them: the two imams' standing, their precedence in sifting, and the ummah's reception of the books, which Ibn Hajar calls stronger than many routes short of tawatur. The exceptions are what a hafiz criticized and what conflicts without a way to prefer.

(6)

Why is 'aziz not a condition of sahih? Because authenticity follows the reliability of the narrators, not their count. "Actions are by intentions" is gharib in several layers and sits at the head of al-Bukhari. Extra narrators help, for instance by lifting jahalah, but they are not a requirement.

(7)

Why doesn't fame make a hadith mashhur? Because technical mashhur is a claim about the chain, more than two at every layer, while fame is a fact about how often people quote it. "Haste is from Shaytan" is famous and weak.

(8)

Commonly Confused

PairThe difference
Mashhur vs. famousA chain structure vs. a social fact. They can come apart either way.
Mashhur vs. sahihNumber of routes vs. grade. A mashhur hadith can be sahih, hasan, or da'if.
'Aziz vs. "exactly two"'Aziz only forbids dropping below two.
Gharib vs. fardSame meaning; by habit fard is used for the mutlaq kind and gharib for the nisbi.
Gharib vs. shadhCount vs. contradiction. A lone reliable narrator who contradicts no one is gharib, not shadh.
Mutawatir lafzi vs. ma'nawiSame wording through every chain vs. the same meaning in varied wording; the second is far more common.
'Ilm daruri vs. 'ilm nazariReached without inference by anyone vs. reached through inference by one capable of it.
Zann in the Quran vs. zann in mustalahBaseless guesswork vs. a preponderance built on real evidence, as Nur al-Din 'Itr points out.

Case 1A report has three narrators from the Companion, two at the next layer, and twenty after that. Classify it.

'Aziz. The narrowest layer has two, and the narrowest layer decides.

Case 2Thousands today report that a certain philosophical claim is true. Is that tawatur?

No. Tawatur must rest on sense perception at its end, and it must be many at every layer back to the source. A shared conclusion of reasoning meets neither condition.

Case 3A hadith has two routes: one is mashhur, the other gharib. What is the hadith, and what happens to the gharib route?

The hadith takes the highest valid classification among its routes, so it is mashhur. The gharib route keeps its own description as a route; it is not overwritten.

Case 4A student says: "Al-Tirmidhi called this hadith da'if, so it is settled forever." What is missing?

Such a judgment is often tawaqquf, suspension on the evidence then available, left open for later scholars. The window to reclassify stays open in principle, though overturning an established consensus is very difficult in practice.

Case 5Someone cites "Seek knowledge even as far as China" as sound because it is mashhur. Answer him in one sentence.

Mashhur describes how many routes it has, not whether they are reliable, and none of this hadith's routes escapes serious criticism.

Questions to Bring to the Shaykh

  • Can a report be mutawatir for one hearer and not for another, given the fourth condition? How do the scholars handle that?
  • Is the knowledge yielded by the Sahihayn's qara'in the same for a specialist and for a layperson?
  • When the Hawd and khuff-wiping hadiths are called mutawatir ma'nawi, how much variation in wording is tolerated before the meaning is no longer "the same"?
