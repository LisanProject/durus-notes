Ghareeb Hadith

Defining al-Ghareeb

وَالرَّابِعُ: الغَرِيبُ: وَهُوَ مَا يَتَفَرَّدُ بِرِوَايَتِهِ شَخْصٌ وَاحِدٌ فِي أَيِّ مَوْضِعٍ وَقَعَ التَّفَرُّدُ بِهِ مِنَ السَّنَدِ.
The fourth category is al-Ghareeb. It is that which a single person is solitary in narrating, at whatever point in the isnad this solitariness occurs.

Al-Ghareeb is a narration in which a single person is solitary at any point in the chain of transmission, whether at the level of the Companion or at any later link.

(1)

Ghareeb divides into two types, listed below.

Ghareeb Mutlaq (absolute) is the case where the solitary narrator occurs at the very origin of the chain, at the Companion level itself.

Ghareeb Nisbi (relative) is the case where the isnad is otherwise well attested and larger than one narrator but narrows to a single narrator at some later point.
