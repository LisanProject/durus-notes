بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ

Welcome to my free online notes documenting my journey through the traditional Islamic sciences. The intent of this site is to provide structured, accessible notes and eventually downloadable references for anyone walking a similar path, whether you are studying formally under a teacher or attending circles at your local mosque.

Anyone who has studied the Islamic sciences seriously runs into the same problem. There is no single place that pulls together clear, well organized notes across the different branches. 'Aqidah, Fiqh, Nahw, Hadith, and the rest are scattered across books, lecture notes, and memory, with no consolidated reference to return to. I wanted a site that felt like it was written by someone who actually wanted you to understand, with clear explanations, organized topics, and a place worth coming back to years later. I hope to do something similar with my website.

The teachers who shaped me, in particular Sidi Shaykh Abdullahi Ahmed, Sidi Shaykh Muhammad al-Maliki, Sidi Shaykh Mahdi Lock, Sidi Shaykh Muhyiddin Lashen, and Sidi Shaykh Ibrahim Niyass Shuru embodied the Sunnah in the way they walked into a room, in the way they treated people, and in the light with which they spoke about the Prophet, peace be upon him. What I have benefited most from them is a deep respect for classical scholarship and a sense of just how much has been preserved and passed down with care.

At present the notes cover Arabic Syntax and Morphology, Fiqh, Usul al-Fiqh, Ash'ari Aqidah, and Hadith, with practice problems and i'rab exercises throughout the Arabic sections. I would like to thank in advance anyone who writes in with corrections. If you find an error, please let me know and I can go ahead and make the appropriate change.

The Ten Foundations of Every Science

A short didactic poem traditionally taught before beginning any Islamic science. It lists the ten matters a student should know before diving into a subject, such as its definition, topic, and benefit.

إِنَّ مَبادِئَ كُلِّ فَنٍّ عَشَرَهْ     الحَدُّ وَالمَوضوعُ ثُمَّ الثَّمَرَهْ

وفَضلُهُ وَنِسبَةٌ وَالواضِعُ     وَالاِسمُ الاِستِمدادُ حُكمُ الشَّارِعِ

مَسائِلٌ وَالبَعضُ بِالبَعضِ اكتَفى     وَمَن دَرى الجَميعَ حازَ الشَّرَفا

The foundations of every science are ten:
definition, subject matter, then its fruit

its virtue, its relation to other sciences, its founder,
its name, its sources, and the ruling of the Lawgiver on studying it

and its issues. Some suffice with only some of these,
but whoever knows them all has attained the honor of true mastery.

Try memorizing these three lines before moving on insha'Allah. They take a few minutes, and having them by heart makes it much easier to orient yourself whenever you pick up a new subject for the first time.
